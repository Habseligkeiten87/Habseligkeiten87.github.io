One world, four perspectives

A 3D/4D world model must reason about space, reconstruct dynamic scenes, support embodied action, and be protected against misuse.

The four sections below address these requirements in turn: spatial reasoning, 3D/4D reconstruction, embodied navigation and safety. Each opens a page with the papers, figures, posters and demos.

01Spatial reasoning空间推理

How can a model ground language in 3D space, and from whose viewpoint?

Most 3D vision-language benchmarks name the target object, so a model can succeed by matching names instead of reasoning about space. I build benchmarks that remove this shortcut and methods that bring the reasoning of 2D multimodal LLMs into 3D without any 3D annotation.

023D/4D reconstruction三维/四维重建

What in a scene changes over time, what stays, and how to reconstruct?

A dynamic scene mixes structure that stays fixed with content that moves. I develop Gaussian splatting methods that keep the two apart, from semantic-guided reconstruction of urban scenes to physically grounded world models that turn multi-view video into simulation-ready environments.

03Embodied navigation具身导航

How can an embodied agent follow long-horizon instructions in unseen environments and on real robots?

Vision-and-language navigation requires an agent to track its progress through an instruction, ground the landmarks that matter for the current subgoal, and understand the geometry around them before it acts. At AgiBot X-Lab I lead this research for the Lingxi X2 and A3 Ultra humanoids, from representation guidance and test-time memory to deployment on the robot.

04Safety of 3D/4D generative models生成安全

As videos become reconstructable 4D assets, how can the content they capture be protected?

3D Gaussian splatting and feed-forward 4D reconstruction make captured content easy to reconstruct, fine-tune and redistribute. I study proactive protection across the asset life cycle: source videos, 4D Gaussian assets, and the 3D generative models built from them.

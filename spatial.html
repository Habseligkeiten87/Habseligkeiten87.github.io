01Spatial reasoning空间推理

How can a model ground language in 3D space, and from whose viewpoint?

Most 3D vision-language benchmarks name the target object in the query, so a model can succeed by matching names instead of reasoning about space. My work studies language-guided spatial reasoning in 3D scenes: relative position, absolute distance, narrative and parametric perspective, and the viewpoint a query assumes.

SURPRISE3D defines 3D spatial reasoning segmentation and provides 200k+ queries over 900+ ScanNet++ scenes with no object names. MLLM-For3D transfers the reasoning of frozen 2D multimodal LLMs to 3D segmentation without any 3D annotation. EgoAudit tests whether 3D vision-language models infer a speaker's viewpoint or only follow one they are given.

SURPRISE3D: A Dataset for Spatial Understanding and Reasoning in Complex 3D Scenes

Jiaxin Huang*, Ziwen Li*, Hanlue Zhang*, Runnan Chen, Zhengqing Gao, Xiao He, Yandong Guo, Wenping Wang, Tongliang Liu, Mingming Gong

NeurIPS 2025, Datasets & Benchmarks Track · first author, project leader

arXivProceedingsProject pageCodeDatasetPoster (PDF)

A benchmark for 3D spatial reasoning segmentation: 200k+ vision-language pairs over 900+ ScanNet++ scenes, including 89k+ human-written spatial queries that name no object. Spatial queries cover relative position, absolute distance, and narrative and parametric perspective; knowledge queries cover common sense and human intention.

C6

MLLM-For3D: Adapting Multimodal Large Language Model for 3D Reasoning Segmentation

Jiaxin Huang, Runnan Chen, Ziwen Li, Zhengqing Gao, Xiao He, Yandong Guo, Mingming Gong, Tongliang Liu

NeurIPS 2025 · first author

arXivProceedingsProject pageCodePoster (PDF)

The first label-free framework for 3D reasoning segmentation. A frozen 2D multimodal LLM segments several posed views from one implicit query; token-for-query alignment and a spatial consistency strategy turn the multi-view masks into reliable pseudo-labels that train a 3D encoder without any 3D annotation.
